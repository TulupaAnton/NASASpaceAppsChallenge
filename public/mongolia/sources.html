Mongolia data and methodology

Choose a reference city for an aimag or the capital to explore the bundled 2005–2024 NASA POWER monthly series. A reference is one grid location, so its results can differ from conditions elsewhere in the aimag. Exact-coordinate analysis fetches upstream grid data for the selected land point.

Climate and energy

NASA POWER provides MERRA‑2 temperatures and wind, CERES incoming solar irradiation, and an elevation value for the grid point. Wind at 100 m is estimated from the 10 m and 50 m wind series with an elevation-based air-density adjustment for wind power density. The 2025–2034 climate scenario extends the fitted seasonal trend.

Screening score

The Mongolian references and exact points use V6’s fixed screening thresholds, with 30% for climate and cooling and 70% for solar and wind. Water access and discharge are outside the scoring scope. Rankings cover the 22 Mongolian reference locations.

Area estimates

Bundled mode uses the reference cities within the radius. Live mode uses up to five land sample coordinates and reports native 50 m wind. Several coordinates can share a NASA grid cell. The panel shows sample coverage for each result.
