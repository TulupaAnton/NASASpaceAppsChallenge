Canada data & sources

Choose a province or territory’s reference city to explore bundled 2005–2024 NASA POWER monthly series, or click any land coordinate on the map for live analysis. Because Canada and Finland share the same MERRA-2 wind pipeline and fixed physical thresholds, locations in both countries can be compared side by side.

Screening methodology

Both Canadian reference sites and exact-coordinate queries use fixed screening weights: 25% for climate and cooling demand, 60% for solar and wind renewable resources, and 15% for modeled water indicators. Screening estimates do not replace local grid, permitting, or water-rights studies.

Primary data sources

Monthly air temperatures, incoming solar irradiation, and 100 m wind estimates (1984–2024), plus 2025–2034 seasonal trend scenarios.
