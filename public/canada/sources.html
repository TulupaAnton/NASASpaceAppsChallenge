Canada data and methodology

Choose a province or territory’s reference city to explore the bundled 2005–2024 NASA POWER monthly series. A reference is one grid location, so its results can differ from conditions elsewhere in the province or territory. Exact-coordinate analysis fetches upstream grid data for the selected land point.

Climate and energy

NASA POWER provides MERRA‑2 temperatures and wind, CERES incoming solar irradiation, and an elevation value for the grid point. Wind at 100 m is estimated from the 10 m and 50 m wind series with an elevation-based air-density adjustment for wind power density. The 2025–2034 climate scenario extends the fitted seasonal trend.

Screening score

The Canadian references and exact points use V6’s fixed screening thresholds: 25% for climate and cooling, 60% for solar and wind, and 15% for modeled water indicators. Water inputs use Copernicus GloFAS v4 and Copernicus DEM GLO-90 through a 49-point stencil. These estimates do not establish project water access. Rankings cover the 13 Canadian reference locations.
