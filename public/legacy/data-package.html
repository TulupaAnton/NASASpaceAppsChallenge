Data & sources

DataX evaluates data-center siting candidates across Finland and Canada using a unified, transparent physical screening model and open Earth observation datasets.

Assembled Finland pipeline Monthly CSV Annual CSV

Unified screening methodology

All regions and live coordinates share fixed screening weights: 25% climate and cooling demand, 60% solar and wind renewable potential, and 15% modeled water indicators. Because both Finland and Canada use NASA POWER MERRA-2 wind estimates and physical thresholds, scores can be compared directly across countries.

Regional rankings compare 19 Finnish reference sites and 13 Canadian reference sites. Screening scores support early-stage siting; final project decisions also require grid capacity, permitting, fiber connectivity, and local water-rights verification.
