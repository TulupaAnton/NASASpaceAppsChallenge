National, provincial and territorial boundaries

Source: Statistics Canada, 2021 Census cartographic boundary files. The 13 provincial and territorial features are simplified for display, with coastal islands smaller than 2 km² omitted. These adapted boundaries are for geographic selection.

Map preparation

The image and boundaries share a spherical Lambert conformal conic projection with standard parallels at 49° and 77° N and central meridian at 96° W. City markers are geographic landmarks; they do not indicate available analysis datasets.
